How it started

One burger. One tiny kitchen. A whole lot of crunch.

Hot & Spicy began in 2018 with a borrowed fryer, a handwritten menu and a stubborn belief that fast food could still be made with care. Our first shop in Bagar had eight seats — and a queue that kept getting longer.

Today, we're still independent, still obsessed with the details, and still mixing every sauce in-house. The menu has grown, but the promise hasn't changed: real ingredients, generous portions and food worth slowing down for.

— The Hot & Spicy crew
